Where this starts

My family runs a wholesale dal and cereal shop in Etawah. I grew up around sacks, scales and a ledger that had to balance every single evening — nobody there cared how clever the method was, only whether the number was right.

That's still how I build software. Correctness first, then speed, then polish.
